Founder

A Personal Mission. A Purpose Bigger Than Me.

The story behind Suhood — by Syed Furqan, Founder.

“Suhood began with a simple intention: to help Muslims reconnect with their faith in a world full of distractions.

I believe technology should do more than keep us scrolling. It should help us become more mindful of our time, more consistent in our worship, and more intentional about the way we live.

That belief inspired Suhood — a faith-centered experience designed to bring Salah, Quran, Dhikr, and mindful focus into everyday life.

My vision isn't simply to build another app. It's to create a tool that encourages small, consistent steps towards a better relationship with Allah, without turning worship into a competition or a source of pressure.

Suhood is a journey, and this is only the beginning.”

Syed Furqan Founder of Suhood

Privacy & trust

Permissions with purpose.

Suhood asks only for what features need. Review the full draft policy before relying on it publicly.

Local-first habits

Core progress, preferences, and many worship tools are stored on your device. Some features (for example online Quran audio or dua sources) may use the network when you choose them.

Optional system access

Focus and Salah-time protection may require Accessibility, Usage Access, notifications, and related settings. You can decline features that need them.
