What is Suhood

A personal Deen companion for everyday life.

Suhood is built for Muslims who want structure without pressure — Salah reminders, Quran reading, Dhikr, Focus sessions, and a personalized Worship Quest that grows with you.

It is designed to feel quiet and intentional: dark Hybrid Glass interface, clear navigation, and tools that support consistency rather than guilt. Your progress stays on your device; you stay in control.

Features

Everything you need for daily Deen.

Real screens from the app. Each feature is designed to fit into ordinary days — not to overwhelm them.

Salah times & home overview

See the next prayer at a glance, track today’s progress, and open Worship, Alarm, Quran, and More from a single calm home screen.

Location-aware times help you stay oriented through the day. A Worship Quest bar shows your current challenge progress without noise.

Quran — reading & Mushaf

Browse 114 surahs, continue where you left off, set a daily reading goal, and open Madinah-style Mushaf pages with clear Arabic text.

Translation view supports deeper reading. Audio controls are available when you want to listen along.

Dhikr, Tasbeeh & Duas

A focused Tasbeeh counter with loop tracking, and a searchable Hisn al-Muslim collection for duas by moment and purpose.

Built for short, sincere sessions — whether you have one minute or ten.

Focus & Salah-time protection

Choose apps that distract you. During configured Salah windows, Suhood can interrupt those apps and invite you back to prayer with a full-screen lock experience.

Effectiveness depends on Android permissions (Accessibility, Usage Access, and related settings) and can vary by device manufacturer. Suhood never claims perfect system-level control.

Alarms with wake-up challenges

Set alarms with optional math, tap-sequence, or memory challenges so dismissing them takes a moment of attention — useful for Fajr and early mornings.

Alarm delivery is subject to Android battery and background limits. Keep Suhood unrestricted in battery settings for best results.

Qibla

A clean compass view with location context and clear turn guidance. Hold the phone flat and rotate until the needle meets the Kaaba marker.

Privacy & trust

Permissions with purpose.

Suhood asks only for what features need. Review the full draft policy before relying on it publicly.

Local-first habits

Core progress, preferences, and many worship tools are stored on your device. Some features (for example online Quran audio or dua sources) may use the network when you choose them.

Optional system access

Focus and Salah-time protection may require Accessibility, Usage Access, notifications, and related settings. You can decline features that need them.
