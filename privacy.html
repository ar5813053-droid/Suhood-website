Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated for website launch preparation

This is an editable draft based on typical Suhood app behaviour. Review and approve against the actual shipped Android build before presenting it as final legal text.

Suhood (“the app”) is an Android application designed to support Islamic daily habits such as Salah awareness, Quran reading, Dhikr, Focus tools, alarms, and related features. This policy describes how information may be handled when you use the app or this website.

Information on your device

Core preferences, onboarding answers, worship progress, selected apps for Focus, and similar settings are stored locally on your device. You can clear app data through Android system settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications — reminders for Salah, quests, and alarms.
  • Location — prayer times and Qibla direction.
  • Usage Access / Accessibility — optional, for Focus and Salah-time app protection features.
  • Display over other apps — optional, for lock or challenge overlays where enabled.
  • Exact alarms — optional, for more reliable alarm scheduling where Android allows it.

Features that depend on a permission will not work fully if that permission is denied or revoked.

Network use

Some optional content (for example online Quran audio sources or online dua catalogues) may require an internet connection when you use those features. The website itself does not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children

Suhood is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support channels, contact us.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support.suhood@gmail.com
